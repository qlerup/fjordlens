`attraction_catalog.json` contains derived OpenStreetMap park boundaries for
Bakken, Legoland, BonBon-Land, Knuthenborg Safaripark and Københavns Zoo.
Retrieved 2026-09-06 through the OSM API. Object identifiers are included per park.

Regenerate with `python scripts/build_attraction_catalog.py`. Coordinates outside
these five mapped parks continue to use the ordinary cached online lookups.
